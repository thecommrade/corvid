## Main-session notes

- The Phase 1 criterion (package spec §7 / ADR-0004): weights + KV cache at the chosen quantisation exceed the largest single node's VRAM + free RAM (≈ 6 GB + 19 GB ≈ 25 GB). Candidates below that exceed only 8 GB VRAM are the weaker fallback list.
- Model files live on the hub's storage pool (path recorded in R00/Phase 1 plan); download once, serve from there; `-c` cache on workers avoids re-streaming weights at every load.

## Recommendations for the spec

1. Phase-1 demo model: Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B at Q8_0, file ggml-org/Qwen3.8-27B-GGUF/Qwen3.8-27B-Q8_0.gguf (28,595,763,552 B = 28.60 GB = 26.6 GiB). With -c 8192 the footprint is ≈29.3 GB (≈27.3 GiB): above the ~25 GB single-node ceiling, ~11 GB beyond the pool's 18 GB VRAM, far below the ≈81 GB pool. Apache-2.0, ungated, AA Intelligence Index v4.1.1 = 52 (#1/137 in its class, 2026-08-22). Record the AA snapshot date and Arena snapshot date in the ADR as the named ranking basis.
