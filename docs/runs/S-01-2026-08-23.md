## Conclusions (feed R01)

1. **Wi-Fi is the ceiling, not WireGuard:** tailnet ≈ LAN throughput on every measured pair
   (sometimes better); encryption overhead is invisible at these rates.
2. **The hub's send side is the fleet's weakest link** (~40–64 Mbit/s). First-load streaming
   of a 27 GB model from the hub to workers runs ~ an hour; the workers' `-c` cache makes it
   one-time. Re-plugging the hub's Ethernet is the single highest-value network change.
3. **RTT 5–10 ms avg with 20–90 ms Wi-Fi outliers.** RPC decode calibration (SmolLM2-135M,
   3-node split, llama-bench): local-hub tg 296.7 t/s (3.4 ms/tok) vs split tg 31.75 t/s
   (31.5 ms/tok) → **+28 ms/token ≈ 4 blocking round-trips at the measured ~7 ms RTT**,
   consistent with R01-F24's ≥2-RTT-per-boundary model (2 remote workers). pp was unaffected
   (267.7 vs 251.4 — parallel prefill hides latency).
4. All pairs run **direct** after F1/F2 (see `docs/runs/phase-0-2026-08-23.md`); pre-fix,
   optiplex→ahnoway was DERP(iad) at ~256 ms — a 30× RTT penalty that would have made decode
   ~1 s/token. Direct paths are not optional for RPC inference.
5. Cross-house path remains `UNVERIFIED` (ADR-0004 follow-on at first member).
